Reproducible GIS Workflows & Metadata

Methodology & Spatial Data Sources

Step 01

Geodetic Harmonization

All Sri Lankan vector and raster layers are standardized to SLD99 / Sri Lanka Grid 1999 (EPSG:5235) or Kandawala (EPSG:5234) for accurate metric area and hydraulic calculations.

Step 02

Hydro-Conditioning & DEM QA

LiDAR and ALOS PALSAR 12.5m elevation models undergo pit-filling, stream burning, and cross-section extraction prior to HEC-HMS & HEC-RAS simulations.

Step 03

Cartographic & ISO 19115 Standards

Every map output includes verified scale bars, coordinate graticules, hierarchical legends, and documented data lineage.
